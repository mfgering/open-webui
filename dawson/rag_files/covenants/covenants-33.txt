COVENANTS: Article IV : Section 4.3: paragraph a: Upper Boundary: The horizontal plane of the top surface of the wallboard in the ceilings within the Unit. As depicted on the Plans, the ceilings in certain portions of the Unit may be at different elevations. In such cases, the upper boundary of such Unit shall not be a single horizontal plane but shall vary with the differing finished ceiling elevations within different portions of the Unit.
