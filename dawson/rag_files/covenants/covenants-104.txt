COVENANTS: Article XII : Section 12.6: paragraph b: Proceeds on account of damage to Units shall be held in the following undivided shares: (1) When the damage is to be restored, for the Owners of damaged Units in proportion to the cost of repairing the damage to each such Owner’s Unit, which cost shall be determined by the Association. (2) When the damage is not to be restored, an undivided share for each Owner, such share being the same as each such Owner’s allocated Common Elements Interest.
